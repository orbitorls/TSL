## 2025-06-16 - Fast Feature Extraction
**Learning:** `extract_features_from_landmark_df` and `extract_features` functions were identified as performance bottlenecks. Using iterative column-by-column mean extraction with pandas Series `safe_mean(lm_df[col])` takes around 11 seconds for 1000 iterations.
**Action:** Replace iterative extraction with vectorized Pandas/NumPy operations. Calculate means for all columns at once and convert the series to dictionary for fast index lookup using `.mean(numeric_only=True).fillna(0.0).to_dict()`, reducing time to ~1.5s for `extract_features` and ~0.35s for `extract_features_from_landmark_df` (when combined with caching). Note: `extract_features` is in `src/core/features.py` and `extract_features_from_landmark_df` in `src/data/feature_extraction.py`.
