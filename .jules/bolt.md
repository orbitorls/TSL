## 2024-05-19 - Pandas Vectorized Extraction vs Iterative Safe Mean
**Learning:** In Pandas DataFrame feature extraction for sequences (like in `src/data/feature_extraction.py` and `src/core/features.py`), using iterative calls to series methods like `safe_mean(lm_df[col])` or `lm_df[col].fillna(0.0).to_numpy()` per column introduces massive overhead due to repeated Pandas Series creation and string lookups.
**Action:** When extracting sequence features or computing means across numerous columns, identify available columns first (`lm_df.columns.intersection(col_list)`), then use vectorized operations like `lm_df[cols].mean(numeric_only=True).fillna(0.0).to_dict()` or bulk NumPy assignment via advanced indexing (`pd.Index(col_list).get_indexer(available_cols)`). This approach yields up to a ~100x speedup in sequence extraction.
