## 2024-05-18 - Pandas Iteration Bottleneck
**Learning:** `extract_features_from_landmark_df` and `extract_sequence_from_landmark_df` iterate over columns row-by-row and use `safe_mean(lm_df[col])`, causing severe overhead due to Panda's iterative overhead on `.mean()` for single columns and exception handling in `safe_mean`. This results in feature extraction taking ~1.1s for simple dummy datasets.
**Action:** Use vectorized `.mean().fillna(0.0).to_dict()` and batch extraction into NumPy arrays. This resulted in a ~30x to ~40x speedup (1.1s -> 0.03s). Apply this optimization to both sequence extraction and single-vector extraction. Also apply similar optimization to `src/data/loader.py`'s `_extract_162_features` and `_extract_162_sequence` and `src/core/features.py`'s `extract_features`.
